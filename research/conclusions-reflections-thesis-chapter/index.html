Conclusions and Reflections

Tanzhe Tang

Thesis chapter · Delft University of Technology · 2022

Download the standalone chapter PDF View the full dissertation

Abstract

This chapter concludes the efforts made in the thesis to achieve its research goals, with a special focus on the relations between the proposed methods. It identifies promising directions for future work, discusses the societal relevance of the thesis, and reflects on the field of opinion dynamics and polarization.

How to cite

Tang, T. (2022). Conclusions and reflections. In T. Tang, On the Methods for Explaining Polarization of Private and Unobservable Opinions: An Opinion-Behavior Co-Evolutionary Approach (Chapter 6, pp. 155–176). Delft University of Technology.
