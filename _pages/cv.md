### Teaching & Student Supervision

At the University of Amsterdam, I serve as a Teaching Assistant for the MSc Computational Science course *Agent-Based Modelling*. In 2022 and 2023, I designed, coordinated, and lectured the *Opinion Dynamics on the Internet* bachelor's course at the University of Groningen. I have also lectured at the GESIS Spring Seminar on *Simulation Techniques in the Social Sciences* at the Leibniz Institute for the Social Sciences, and previously served as a Teaching Assistant for *Mathematics for Economics I* at the University of Leicester. 

Furthermore, I actively supervise Master's students, including recent dissertations at the University of Amsterdam and Delft University of Technology.

### Research Visits & Invited Talks

In 2019, I completed a research visit at the Department of Social and Political Science at the University of Milan, hosted by Prof. Flaminio Squazzoni and Dr. Federico Bianchi. I have also been invited to present my research at several institutions, including ETH Zurich (2023), the University of Milan (2024), and Zhejiang Gongshang University (2026).

### Grants & Academic Service

I am the Founder and Chair of the Computational Modelling and Artificial Intelligence for Social System (CMAISS) conference, part of the International Conference on Computational Science. Throughout my postdoctoral work, I have been an active participant in major funded research initiatives, including the ERC Consolidator grant BEHAVE, the NWO Open Competition SpinOut, and the ORA project ToRealSim. I regularly serve as a reviewer for journals such as the *Journal of Artificial Societies and Social Simulation* and *Quality & Quantity*.
