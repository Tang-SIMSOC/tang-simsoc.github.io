The Computational Modeling and Artificial Intelligence for Social Systems ([CMAISS](https://www.iccs-meeting.org/iccs2026/workshops/#cmaiss)) workshop will be held in Hamburg together with the International Conference on Computational Science (29 June - 1 July).
[Jaeyoung Kwak](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=jj4oWqEAAAAJ&hl=en) (NTU) and I will co-chair the workshop.
