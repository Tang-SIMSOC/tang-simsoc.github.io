On 29 June, the 2nd Computational Modeling and Artificial Intelligence for Social Systems ([CMAISS](https://www.iccs-meeting.org/iccs2026/workshops/#cmaiss)) workshop took place at DESY Hamburg, together with the International Conference on Computational Science.

This year, the workshop featured two sessions with eight presentations. We were also delighted that one of the workshop presentations, by Soveatin Kuntur, won second place in the ICCS student competition.
